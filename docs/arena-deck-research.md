# Arena tournament deck research

November 16, 2026 · cr-rl-arena `21615a8`

## Summary

1. **Elixir Golem is the biggest economy change.** It costs 3 and splits into seven total bodies without paying the opponent any elixir. Battle Healer can heal the push while walking and without attacking. Start with **Healer Elixir Golem**.
2. **Miner hits towers for 195 versus 39 live, and X-Bow prioritizes towers over distracting troops.** Test **Miner Wall Breakers cycle** and **Tower-first X-Bow** against defenders that react to both threats.
3. **Graveyard spawned 20 Skeletons and the arena's Princess Towers are weaker.** Test **Graveyard Hut** alongside siege. The extra Skeletons and independent Hut spawns give it pressure without needing a champion ability.
4. **Replace real-game spell assumptions.** Arrows hit once for 123, Barbarian Barrel produced no Barbarian, and a 3-elixir Royal Delivery can land on the enemy tower for 134. Test **Hog Delivery cycle** and **Giant anti-rush** to see whether cheap splash and direct chip convert into wins.
5. **Air pressure carries over.** Minion Giant is in the card pool at 169 damage per hit versus the live game's 153. **Minion Giant cycle** and **LavaLoon anti-air** put two different air win conditions into the shortlist. Early 3× elixir in overtime and the minimum-tower-HP tiebreak give cheap chip and late heavy pushes different paths to a win.

Start the first screening pass with those eight decks, the other four candidates in Part 3, and the retained reference decks. The all-troop bridge client won 7 of 20 against the one-hour network, so first screen for reliable defense against that client and for a win condition that still works against an opponent that defends (`README.md:539–546`). The offline measurements below establish interactions and legality. They give no deck win rates.

### 1. Elixir Golem and Battle Healer — very high

- **Engine.** Elixir Golem costs 3 and splits into 2 Golemites and 4 Blobs, with 4,539 combined listed HP. The generic death handler has no defender elixir grant. Battle Healer pulses 26 HP every 0.25 s in radius 3 to friendly troops including herself, independent of attacks. `gym/data/cards.json:897–954,1792–1810`, `gym/src/engine.rs:1217–1262,1345–1351,1469–1477,2520–2574`. Live Elixir Golem gives the defender 4 elixir across the three stages, and live Battle Healer's passive heal is self-only, with ally healing tied to attacking. [Supercell Elixir Golem rework](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/balance-changes-august-2022-2/), [Battle Healer mechanics](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/season-6-clashmas/).
- **Probe.** Seed 0, left-bridge Elixir Golem with a defending Valkyrie. Seven bodies appeared and were gone by tick 473. Defender elixir was below cap at every death, and each death tick added only the passive ~0.01785. In a second probe, a Fireball damaged a Giant and Battle Healer on their own side. The Giant healed 3,305 → 3,331 → 3,357 at five-tick intervals with no combat nearby.
- **Deck implication.** A cheap, repeatable tank buys the healer and spawners time without financing the counterpush. Screen against splash plus a building and a dedicated tank killer.

### 2. Miner and X-Bow can win without a large push — very high

- **Miner, read + probe.** The 3-elixir deploy-anywhere Miner has 195 damage and the melee hit applies 100% to towers. Casting on the enemy Princess center in an idle-lane probe gave five 195-damage strikes at ticks 36, 62, 88, 114 and 140. Live level-11 Miner deals 39 per Crown Tower hit. Arena tower HP translates to 13 unopposed hits versus 79 in live. This compares cumulative hits across casts. `gym/data/cards.json:994–1008`, `gym/src/engine.rs:437–452,527–532,1689–1705,2200–2214`. [July balance](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/july-balance-changes-2026/), [November Miner data](https://crtool.app/en/units/miner/).
- **Deck implication.** Miner can be the main win condition. Pair him with cheap defenses and make the opponent defend both Miner and another tower threat.

- **Engine.** X-Bow has `prefers_buildings`, 11.5 sight and range, 1600 HP, a 3.5 s deploy and a 40 s lifetime. If it holds a troop, it can rescan for a building. The engine ranks an in-sight building or tower ahead of a closer troop. Mortar lacks this priority, has a 3.5-tile dead zone and splash. `gym/data/cards.json:1197–1230`, `gym/src/engine.rs:1940–1954,1964–1983,1994–2038`. Live X-Bow can be distracted by troops before lock and has a 30 s lifetime. [Supercell siege guide](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/fun/archetype-deck-challenge/), [live X-Bow card data](https://www.royaletools.com/cards/x-bow).
- **Probe.** Seed 0, X-Bow at (3.5, 14.5) from tick 60, defending Knight at (3.5, 14.5) in its own frame from tick 65. With the Knight ~1.10 tiles away on activation, the first 61 damage at tick 168 went to the Princess Tower, matching the idle-lane control. A deeper X-Bow at (3.5, 9.5) hit the Knight first. An untouched X-Bow at (3.5, 8.5) lasted exactly 40 s from placement.
- **Deck implication.** An opposing building can intercept the lock or tank it. Ordinary ground troops can still kill the X-Bow but may fail to buy time for the tower. Give X-Bow cheap ground defense and enough air coverage to survive Balloon.

### 3. Graveyard and fixed, fragile towers — very high

- **Engine.** Graveyard samples a radius-4 disk every 0.5 s for 10 s. The two Princess Towers start at 2,534 HP and deal 90 per hit, about 17% below live level-11 Princess values of 3,052 and 109. The 4,008-HP king and two Princess Towers are fixed for both seats. The lobby accepts only the eight cards and has no tower-troop slot. `gym/data/cards.json:1447–1459,2344–2364`, `gym/src/engine.rs:242–248,1041–1048,1124–1151,1317–1333`, `server/CONNECTING.md:77–95`. Supercell cut live Graveyard to 12 Skeletons in June after a placement rework. [Live Graveyard count](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/june-balance-changes-2026/), [live spawn-pattern update](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/february-balance-changes-26/), [Nov. 9 client tower data](https://crtool.app/en/units/tower-princess/).
- **Probe.** One Graveyard produced 20 unique Skeletons, one every 10 ticks from steps 15 through 205 after the cast. Fresh matches returned 2,534 HP for the Princess Towers and 4,008 HP for each King.
- **Deck implication.** Tank + Graveyard is worth screening before champion variants. Lower tower HP also improves safe chip, while lower tower damage reduces the margin for defending bridge rush with the towers alone.

### 5. Deploy, pocket and formations — high

- **Engine and probe.** After a left Princess falls, the code accepts river rows 15 and 16 as well as land rows 17–20 in that half. The tournament guide advertises only the four land rows. Seed 1, left-bridge Balloon versus idle took the tower by tick 355. Bandit placement on that side at `y=15.5,16.5,20.5` succeeded in separate repeats. `y=21.5` and the other lane at `y=20.5` were rejected without spend or cycle. The policy exposes those six rows when the lane opens. Default placements also lack a footprint exclusion. Cannon at (9.5, 3.5) overlapped its own king footprint and was accepted, and Drill appeared immediately as a deploying building directly inside the enemy Princess's footprint at (3.5, 25.5). Probe results show acceptance, with no claim of a net placement advantage. `gym/src/arena.rs:27–47`, `gym/src/engine.rs:412–453,520–542`, `arena/src/feat.rs:58–61,233–240,402–426`, `server/CONNECTING.md:193–223`.
- **Probe.** Royal Recruits deployed at (3.5, 12.5) produced six in a compact 1.4-tile-wide left-lane block. Live Recruits spread across the arena. Royal Hogs and Three Musketeers also use generic count-based clusters in the sim **(Read)**, so do not count on the live split-lane pressure package. `gym/src/engine.rs:469–487,545–615`, `gym/data/cards.json:239–255,770–789,1599–1615`. [Supercell Royal Recruits demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SDaOmwaYDHw).
- **Deck implication.** Pocket attacks reduce the distance to the next fight after a tower falls. Overlapping buildings, river placements and Drill on-center are candidates for local policy trials. The acceptance checks alone do not establish that a particular tile beats a standard placement.

### 6. Evolutions, champions, Mirror and Clone — high

- **Read.** Only Knight, Skeletons and Royal Giant have evolution links. All three automatically evolve on their third use independently, with no special-slot cap, using a 436-HP shield, eight initial bodies, and splash/knockback on the projectile, respectively. There is no on-hit Skeleton multiplication or radial Royal Giant recoil centered on the shooter. Live decks use an Evo slot, Hero/Champion slot and Wild slot. Live Giant, Mirror and the Evo/Hero variants in Part 2 are additional capabilities. `gym/data/cards.json:5–38,89–123,577–615`, `gym/src/engine.rs:458–517,1748–1778`, `gym/src/cards.rs:624–631,735–737`. [Official slots](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/mid-march-update/), [live Skeletons and Royal Giant mechanics](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/card-evolution-2/), [November variants](https://support.supercell.com/clash-royale/en/articles/whats-new-november-2026.html).
- **Read.** The server allows one champion card. The four-card cycle continues and the ability uses the first active champion in engine order. Archer Queen borrows the general cloak and Rage effects, Skeleton King summons eight without collecting souls, and Golden Knight lunges toward one enemy and hits along the line with no chain. Many sim abilities can fire again after cooldown. In a **probe**, two full-HP Queens coexisted after cycling four cards and deploying the second one, consistent with the ordinary four-card cycle. Live Clash moved to most-recent champion ownership and made most abilities single-use in August 2026. Goblinstein in the sim is a Doctor-driven revive, different from live damage, and the bot excludes that ability from its mask. `gym/src/engine.rs:366–376,622–755,845–897,2785–2798`, `gym/data/cards.json:1010–1080,2006–2029`, `arena/src/track.rs:99–102,249–274,319–326`, `server/CONNECTING.md:83–95,222–223`. [Live champion abilities](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/champions-update-article/), [cycle rework](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/october-update-2025/), [single-use patch](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/final-august-balance-changes-826/).
- **Read + Probe.** Mirror costs the last non-Mirror card's cost +1, with no level boost, and the common placement code advances the copied base card's evolution charge. Clone copies friendly troop entities, including flyers and existing clones, at 1 HP, strips shield, preserves the base max HP, and skips deploy delay. A Knight, Clone, then mirrored Clone gave one original and three clones with max HP 1,766 each. A cloned Elixir Golem killed by Zap spawned two full 763-HP Golemites. Cloned Knight and Battle Healer healed above 1 HP in 65 ticks. Clone retains the same death-spawn and bomb definition. `gym/src/engine.rs:421–460,502–517,1049–1075,1217–1262,2541–2572`, `gym/data/cards.json:2125–2130`, `arena/src/track.rs:225–246`. [Live base and evolved Mirror](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/new-season-king-vs-king/).
- **Deck implication.** Cycle linked base cards for their arena forms. Use champions only if measured ability value beats the nonchampion alternatives. Mirror/Clone interactions deserve a controlled test before committing two slots to them.

### 8. Targeting, knockback, pathing and King activation — medium to high

- **Read.** Spatial locks use eligible enemy hitboxes, sight and leash. Hog, Giant and other building-only attackers rescan for nearer buildings before committing in attack range. Ground units route with a tile grid around solid structures and the river. Air goes straight. Troop collision is mass-weighted, and direct hits use mass-resistant knockback with anchored buildings/towers. Tornado's pull uses mass and the river gate. Fisherman's pull is an on-hit melee displacement, with no ranged hook action. Firecracker self-recoils but her attack is a simple circular splash and knockback, without the live spread pattern. `gym/src/engine.rs:1269–1314,1540–1558,1703–1729,1792–1843,1940–2038,2045–2160,2379–2394,2800–2807,3092–3165`, `gym/data/cards.json:861–876,1732–1750`. No general stuck-unit exploit was verified. The reliable targeting exceptions found were X-Bow's priority and Furnace's building classification.
- **Probe.** Mega Knight dealt 525 area damage immediately on submission while still deploying, against no such damage in the no-play control. It became active 19 ticks after the hit. The same data field sets deploy and jump damage. Live level-11 data lists 430 spawn and 537 jump damage and spawn knockback. `gym/data/cards.json:675–699`, `gym/src/engine.rs:489–493,1560–1579,2182–2198`. [Live Mega Knight data](https://www.royaletools.com/cards/mega-knight).
- **Read.** The old king-activation bug in the gap list is resolved. King starts asleep, wakes when damaged or after losing a Princess, and is eligible to be targeted. One king kill ends the match at three crowns, and mutual same-tick king kills draw. Damage from persistent areas bypasses shields, and direct damage can overflow shield HP into body HP **(Read)**. This reduces the value of Recruits and other shielded swarms into splash. Activated state is absent from the wire entity schema. `gym/src/engine.rs:252–292,1530–1536,1944–1953,1153–1170,2422–2443,2503–2508,2575–2612`, `server/CONNECTING.md:183–191`. **Deck implication.** Do not port live Fisherman activations and precise pull placements without testing. A fast Mega Knight deploy is a powerful reaction to grouped ground pressure if the policy can save seven elixir.

### 9. Overtime, the Collector and the policy's information — medium to high

- **Read + Probe.** Regulation is 180 s, 2× from 120 s, then tied crowns enter up to 120 s of sudden death. Arena uses 3× from the start of overtime. Live uses 2× for the first overtime minute and 3× for the second. Arena converges on the same lowest-tower result as the normal live HP tiebreak when both sides have the same tower damage ordering, with an instant score decision at expiry. A crown lead in overtime wins immediately. If crowns are still tied at tick 6000, the lowest HP among surviving towers loses. One Fireball on a Princess, then idle, won at tick 6000 at 0–0. A Fireball on the King alone drew because that side's lowest tower HP was unchanged. `gym/src/arena.rs:49–66`, `gym/src/engine.rs:1335–1343,2680–2736`. [Live elixir timing](https://support.supercell.com/clash-royale/en/articles/elixir.html).
- **Read + Probe.** Collector can be in the opening hand. It costs 6, lasts 70 s, and grants up to eight +1 pulses, 8.5 s apart after deployment, with no death payment. In seed 0, placement at tick 57 yielded the first pulse at observed tick 246 and expired at tick 1457. The actual bot featurizer's estimate at the first payout was 3.393 against actual 4.392. The README calls inferred elixir an upper bound, but a Collector can make it an underestimate. `gym/data/cards.json:1255–1267`, `gym/src/engine.rs:214–239,317–322,1395–1400,1450–1477,2520–2574`, `arena/src/track.rs:364–391`, `README.md:460–470`. Live's reworked Collector has slower production and a longer lifetime. [Official Collector changes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/november-balance-changes-2/).
- **Read.** The policy has six ticks between decisions. It observes visible units and towers, not shields, statuses, pending spells or areas. The tracker infers opponent plays from arriving units and ignores a matching spawn when a parent stands or just died. Estimated opponent spend can also miss Mirror's surcharge and ability cost. A current absence of a counter in history is weak evidence for casting into it. `README.md:449–487,527–535`, `arena/src/track.rs:315–391,406–442`, `arena/src/feat.rs:393–433`, `server/CONNECTING.md:169–191`. **Deck implication.** Prefer lines with independent, visible threats and defensive redundancy. Test chip decks into very early overtime counterpushes, and heavy decks into active spell pressure on the Collector.
